Our Multi-Modal Social Human Navigation Dataset (MuSoHu) is available on GMU Dataverse, which contains ~50 km, 10 hours, 150 trials, 7 humans of data in the wild and allows future robots to learn socially compliant navigation from humans, natural-born experts at such a task!
